Method note

Fit clean. Calibrate. Replay.

Scaling and detector fitting use clean training normals only. Thresholds come from clean calibration scores; ROC-AUC, average precision, and queue metrics are computed on the untouched labeled holdout. The 100-alert queue is an oracle-budget benchmark; sliders are offline diagnostics, not deployment threshold selection.
