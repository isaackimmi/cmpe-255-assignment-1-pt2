Metric guide

Read the signal

i
S

Support how often

Share of all baskets containing the itemset. A 50% support means it appears in 12 of 24 baskets.

C

Confidence how likely

Among baskets with the left-hand side, the share that also contain the right-hand side.

L

Lift how distinct

Confidence divided by the consequent’s popularity. Lift above 1 suggests a positive association.

↗

Use all three together. Confidence alone can make a popular product look more predictive than it is.
